SUPERSTORE SALES ANALYSIS: SUMMARY FOR MANAGER
Period: January 2015 to December 2018 | Data: 9,800 sales records

1. Business size
Total sales are 2.26 million from 4,922 orders and 793 customers. The average order value is 459.48.

2. Growth
Sales grew from 479,856 in 2015 to 722,052 in 2018. 2017 grew 30.64% and 2018 grew 20.30%. Only 2016 fell (-4.26%).

3. Seasonality
Q4 (October to December) brings 38.52% of sales. November and December are the best months, and February is the weakest.

4. Regions
West (31.4%) and East (29.6%) lead. California and New York alone give about 33% of sales. South is the smallest region (17.2%).

5. Products
Technology is the top category (36.6%). Phones and Chairs are the top sub-categories. Fasteners and Labels are the weakest.

6. Customers
The Consumer segment gives 50.8% of sales. 98% of customers are repeat customers. About half of the customers (393) give 80% of sales. New customers fell to only 11 in 2018.

7. Shipping
Standard Class is used for 60% of orders. The average delivery takes 3.96 days, and it depends on the ship mode, not the region.

8. Recommendations

Stock up and run promotions before Q4.
Push sales in the South and Central regions.
Look after the top customers and reward repeat buyers.
Run campaigns to win new customers, because new customers are falling every year.
Review the very low-selling products, such as Fasteners and Labels.
