SUPERSTORE SALES ANALYSIS: KEY INSIGHTS (2015-2018)

Section 2: Overview

Total sales are about 2.26 million (2,261,536.78) from 4,922 orders and 793 customers.
Average order value is 459.48.
The data covers 4 full years, from 3 Jan 2015 to 30 Dec 2018.
The store sold 1,861 products in 529 cities.

Section 3: Time

Sales grew from 479,856 in 2015 to 722,052 in 2018. 2018 is the best year.
2016 fell 4.26%, then 2017 grew 30.64% and 2018 grew 20.30%.
November (350,162) and December (321,480) are the best months. February (59,371) is the weakest.
Q4 (Oct-Dec) brings 38.52% of yearly sales, and Q1 only 15.53%. Sales are seasonal.
Tuesday has the most orders (926). Thursday has the fewest (262).

Section 4: Geography

West is the top region (710,220, 31.40%). South is the lowest (389,151, 17.21%).
West and East together give about 61% of all sales.
California (446,306) and New York (306,361) alone give about 33% of sales.
New York City (252,463) and Los Angeles (173,420) are the top cities.
West and East grow steadily. South fell 32% in 2016 and then recovered.

Section 5: Products

Technology is the top category (827,456, 36.59%). Furniture is 32.22% and Office Supplies 31.19%.
Phones (327,782) and Chairs (322,823) are the top sub-categories.
Fasteners (3,002), Labels (12,348) and Envelopes (16,128) are the weakest.
Canon image CLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top product (61,600).
All three categories grew from 2015 to 2018.

Section 6: Customers

Sean Miller (25,043) and Tamara Chand (19,052) are the top customers.
Consumer segment gives about half of sales (50.76%). Corporate is 30.44% and Home Office 18.79%.
780 of 793 customers (98.36%) ordered more than once, so repeat customers drive the business.
New customers fell each year: 589 in 2015, 141 in 2016, 52 in 2017 and only 11 in 2018.
Average sales per customer is 2,851.87.

Section 7: Shipping

Standard Class is used for 59.83% of orders. Same Day is only 5.30%.
Average delivery takes 3.96 days.
Delivery time depends on ship mode, not region: Same Day about 0 days, First Class about 2, Second Class about 3, Standard Class about 5.

Section 8: Advanced

Top product in each category: Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier (Technology), Fellowes PB500 Binding Machine (Office Supplies) and HON 5400 Series Task Chairs (Furniture).
The running total reaches 2,261,536.78, which equals total sales.
393 customers (49.56%) give 80% of sales. About half of the customers drive most of the revenue.
Month-over-month change is very volatile, so the seasonal pattern (Q4 peak) is more useful.
